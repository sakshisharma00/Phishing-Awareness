Red flags you can look for next time:

  • Urgency / threats ("account will be locked in 24 hours")
  • Generic greeting ("Dear User" instead of your name)
  • Mismatched or suspicious sender domain
  • Link text that doesn't match the actual URL (hover to check)
  • Requests to "verify" credentials via email link

What to do instead:

  • Don't click — go to the site directly by typing the URL yourself
  • Report the email to IT/security team
  • Verify via a separate, known channel (call IT, not the number in the email)
